Perch

Delete your Perch account

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Perch and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. Email info.perchapp@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Perch.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Perch account.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. If the email does not come from the account's address, we may ask you to confirm that you own it.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, and profile photo
  • Your connections with partners, current and past, and everything shared in them: chat messages, photos, widget notes, widget photos, and countdowns. Your partner loses this history too.
  • Your location, time zone, and widget settings
  • Your push notification tokens and signed-in sessions
  • Pending sign-in codes for your email address

What is kept

  • An internal record that an account was deleted, with its internal ID and counts of the items removed. It does not contain your email address, name, messages, or photos.
  • Copies in our encrypted database backups, which expire and are removed within 30 days.

If we must keep something longer for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons, we keep only what is needed, for as long as it is needed.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Turn off location sharing in Settings. This also deletes the location we have stored for you.
  • Settings → Disconnect ends your connection with your partner, but keeps your message history on our servers. To delete that history, delete your account.
  • Email info.perchapp@gmail.com to ask us to delete specific data.

More detail is in the Perch Privacy Policy.
