Perch

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Perch (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a private two-person messaging and Home Screen widget app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at info.perchapp@gmail.com.

1. Who Perch is for

Perch is designed for two connected people. There is no public feed, no discovery, and no followers. Content you send is meant for your partner only (and, where applicable, for moderation of abuse reports).

Perch is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile

  • Email address — used to sign in with a one-time login code (passwordless).
  • Display name — optional name you choose to show your partner.
  • Time zone — your device time zone, used so your partner can see your local time (you can turn time-zone sharing off).

Messages and media

  • Chat messages you send to your partner.
  • Photos you upload in chat or post to the Photo Widget (stored as image files on our object storage).
  • Widget notes — short notes posted only to the Message Widget (not chat).

Location (optional)

If you turn on location sharing:

  • We may collect your device location to show approximate distance, a coarse city name, and (for weather) your partner’s local temperature.
  • Precise coordinates are encrypted at rest on our servers.
  • Distance and city on widgets/in-app only appear according to your and your partner’s privacy settings.

You can turn location sharing and distance display off in Settings.

Device and notifications

  • Session information needed to keep you signed in.
  • Push notification token (e.g. Expo / Apple push token) so we can notify you of messages or widget posts from your partner.
  • Basic technical logs (e.g. errors, delivery status) to operate and secure the service.

Widget settings

We store your choices about what appears on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (for example, whether to show notes, photos, distance, temperature, and preferred units).

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Pair you with one partner and deliver messages, notes, and photos
  • Power Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets with the content and meta you allow
  • Send login codes and push notifications you expect
  • Show optional presence details (local time, distance, city, temperature)
  • Prevent abuse, debug issues, and maintain the service
  • Comply with law when required

We do not sell your personal information.
We do not use your messages or photos for advertising.

4. Who can see your content

Data Who can see it
Chat messages & chat photos You and your partner
Widget notes & widget photos Your partner (on their widgets / in-app widget screens), according to their settings
Display name, shared time zone Your partner (if you share them)
Location-derived distance / city / weather Your partner, only if both of you enable the relevant sharing/display settings
Email Used for your login; not shown to your partner as a contact list

If you file or receive an abuse report, limited account and content information may be reviewed by an administrator to handle the report.

5. Service providers

We use infrastructure providers to run Perch, for example:

  • Cloud hosting and databases
  • Object storage for photos (e.g. DigitalOcean Spaces or similar)
  • Email delivery for login codes
  • Push notification delivery (e.g. Apple / Expo)
  • Weather data for temperature (e.g. Open-Meteo), using approximate location when sharing is on

These providers process data only to provide their services to us, under their own security and privacy terms.

6. Data retention

  • We keep account, message, and media data while your account is active and as needed to provide Perch.
  • Login codes are short-lived and stored as hashes, not as the raw code.
  • If you disconnect from a partner or delete content where the product allows it, we stop showing that content to the other person as designed.
  • You may request account deletion by contacting info.perchapp@gmail.com. We will delete or anonymize personal data unless we must retain something for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures appropriate to a small app of this type, including:

  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS)
  • Hashed login codes
  • Encrypted storage of precise location payloads
  • Access controls for administrative tools

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a device passcode / biometrics and keep your email account secure.

8. Your choices

In the app you can typically:

  • Edit your display name
  • Turn time-zone sharing on or off
  • Turn location sharing and distance display on or off
  • Control what appears on widgets (notes, photos, overlays, units)
  • Sign out of devices
  • Disconnect from your partner

You can also email us to ask what data we hold or to request deletion.

9. International users

Perch may be hosted in a country different from yours. By using the app, you understand your information may be processed in the United States or other regions where our providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, when changes are material, provide a reasonable notice (for example in the app or by email). Continued use after the update means you accept the revised policy.
